**Sizing arithmetic for the aggressive plan:** capital × risk% ÷ (entry − stop). For NPR 1,000,000 at 1.5% risk, that is 15,000 ÷ (2,615 − 2,565 = 50 points, about 1.9% of price), or roughly NPR 785,000 on an index-like stop. That is 78% of capital in one idea, so the risk formula is not the binding limit. Cap any single position at about 25% of capital, and use each stock's own ATR, which is wider than the index's.

**Instruments:** the index itself is not directly tradable. Use a diversified stock basket or mutual funds as proxies, and apply these levels loosely.
